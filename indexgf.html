SETTINGS

Field view

Both views play from behind the offense, Blitz-style. Vertical is a tall portrait screen; horizontal is widescreen, held sideways. On a phone the game rotates to match.

Passing

Drag & release: pull back anywhere like a slingshot and let go. Receiver buttons: three Blitz-style buttons under your QB, ordered like your receivers line up. Tap one to throw, timed for when its receiver flashes green.

Graphics

Retro renders at a low arcade resolution with chunky pixels, like the late-'90s cabinets. Sharp renders at full screen resolution.

      HOW TO PLAY
      Receiver buttons (if set): three buttons under the QB, left to right in the same order the receivers line up. The matching symbol floats over each receiver; green means open. Keys 1–3.
      Drag passing: after the snap, press anywhere, pull back like a slingshot toward your own end, aim the yellow marker at a receiver, release to throw. Green rings are open receivers.
      Running: hold and drag anywhere to move; push past the dashed outer ring for turbo. Runners cut on their own; buttons appear for hurdle and dive. Let go and your player keeps going on his own.
      Defense: tap a defender to take control, then drag to chase. Hurdle jumps to swat a pass. Dive lunges for the tackle.
      Keys: arrows/WASD move · Space turbo · J/L cut · I hurdle · K dive.
